# Phase 7B verification notes

Scope: Advanced scenario comparison (`compare_scenarios`, `compare_payload`), forecast-based simulation (`baseline_source: "forecast"`), deterministic double-counting prevention, FastAPI example routes (`POST /api/simulate`, `POST /api/simulate/compare`), and sample payloads.
Scope constraint: **STOP after Phase 7B. Phase 8 is NOT implemented.**

---

## 1. What was implemented

| Component | Implementation Details |
|---|---|
| **Scenario comparison** | `compare_scenarios(records, scenarios, config=None)` in `ai_engine/scenario_simulator.py`. Supports multi-building, multi-resource (electricity, water, waste), percentage reductions, and campus-wide scopes. Produces portfolio totals, rankings (by resource savings and reduction percentage), assumptions, warnings, and data quality indicators. |
| **Deterministic deduplication policy** | Documented `deterministic_maximum_savings` policy. Identifies overlapping scenarios (same building/resource or campus vs building) and selects the non-overlapping subset that maximizes total savings. Reports `portfolio_savings`, naive `unadjusted_gross_savings`, `included_scenario_ids`, `excluded_scenario_ids`, and structured `overlap_groups`. |
| **Forecast-based scenarios** | Integrates the Phase 6 forecasting engine (`forecast_consumption`) as an optional baseline source (`baseline_source: "forecast"`). Propagates empirical prediction intervals into baseline, savings, projected consumption, and CO2e uncertainty bounds without fabricating arbitrary confidence intervals or unconfigured emission factors. Handles insufficient forecast data via `not_calculated_reason: "insufficient_forecast_data"`. |
| **Contract envelope** | `compare_payload(payload, config=None)` providing standard HTTP 200/422/413/500 JSON envelopes with `simulation_contract_version: "1.0"`. |
| **FastAPI integration** | Added `POST /api/simulate` and `POST /api/simulate/compare` (retaining `/api/v1/ai/...` backward-compatible aliases) in `examples/fastapi_integration/app.py`. |
| **Sample payloads** | Added `sim_compare_multiple_buildings.json`, `sim_compare_overlapping_scenarios.json`, and `sim_forecast_baseline_electricity.json` with deterministic `.response.json` files generated by `scripts/generate_simulation_samples.py --outputs`. |

## 3. Scope guard & limitations

1. **STOP after Phase 7B**: No Phase 8 features, modules, or exports were created. Verified by automated scope guard test in `test_scenario_integration.py`.
2. **Projected vs. Measured**: The engine strictly enforces `projection_type: "hypothetical"` and `measured_savings: null`. Simulator outputs represent target projections, not measured reality.
3. **No financial values**: No currency or monetary savings are ever estimated or reported.
4. **No fabricated intervals or emission factors**: When backtest residuals or emission factors are absent, uncertainty bounds and CO2e values remain strictly `null`.
